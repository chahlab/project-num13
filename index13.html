The top three book series

shatter me series

Shatter Me is a compelling dystopian series that explores power, identity, and resistance through the journey of a young woman whose extraordinary abilities make her both a weapon and a threat in a fractured society.

codename anastasia

Codename: Anastasia is a riveting espionage thriller distinguished by its complex characters, psychological suspense, and a meticulously crafted narrative filled with secrets, deception, and high-stakes conflict.

fourth wing

Fourth Wing is a gripping fantasy novel set in a brutal dragon-rider academy, where ambition, resilience, and hidden secrets determine who survives.
